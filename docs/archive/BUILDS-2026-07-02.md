Goal: bring the in-game clock toward 1:1 real time (start of night: 1 clock second
took 2-2.5s in menus/light scenes, and real fights ran far slower than that once
properly measured). Audio and video quality preserved — every audio-core change was

## What changed under the hood (each its own commit, pushed to origin/samsho2-bringup)

68k side: dispatch loop slimmed 10→6 instructions with exact IRQ/clock semantics
(81e7d26); fused native execution of the per-frame `move.w (A0)+,(VRAM port)` sprite
uploads and RAM fill loops that previously took one TLB exception per word (ea6a4c8).
Audio side (all WAV-bit-exact): synth lookup tables shrunk to fit the VR4300's 8KB
dcache (73204cf, 4b8e417), envelope-generator slots skipped when provably idle this
tick (110f2ce), LFO vibrato frequency stack cached per note/LFO-step (7f30c76),
streamed-ADPCM fetch inlined (d1d3b93). Plus: ADDQ/SUBQ testsuite coverage
(d17aced), clean-build fixes (5f0c57a), and one negative result worth remembering:
-O3 on the audio files hurts ~40% on N64 (icache bloat) — reverted in 4b29c4d.

## Honest status vs the 1:1 goal

Tonight = +67% in-match speed with quality untouched, but true 1:1 (60fps) needs a
step change on the audio side — the YM2610+Z80 must synthesize real-time audio and
still costs ~45-60% of wall time. The identified path: RSP offload of the YM2610
(big job), then further 68k work (MOVE/Bcc fast paths) and render-side wins. See
PLAN-OPTIMIZATION.md (2026-07-02 section) for the ranked next steps.
